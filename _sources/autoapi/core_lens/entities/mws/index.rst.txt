   Microwatershed (MWS) entity.

   Backed by:
   - Static: mws/static (partitioned by year)
   - Annual: mws/annual (partitioned by year)
   - SubAnnual: mws/sub_annual (partitioned by basin then year)

   All paths are relative to the AoI ``data_root``.
